What we make

Three Bags, Ten Colourways

Nivelle is a small catalogue on purpose. Rather than a new drop every month, there are three bags — the Nivelle Luxury Bag, the Fluffy Bunny Purse and the Nivelle Tote Bag — each offered in a handful of colours, between $39.99 and $49.99.

When a colourway sells out, it says so on the product page rather than quietly taking your order. Three of the four tote colours have already gone.
